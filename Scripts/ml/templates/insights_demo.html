UPBI Insights

Phase 1 demo at constituency level, built from public election results.

What this is. A working slice of Phase 1: constituency results (assembly 2012, 2017, 2022 and Lok Sabha 2024 by assembly segment) turned into types, unusual-result flags and an expected range. There are no booth-level results, no news and no prediction here. Types are automatic descriptions of vote history, not reasons people vote.
