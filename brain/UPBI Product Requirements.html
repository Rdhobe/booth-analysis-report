Boundaries we hold.

This system touches sensitive data. These limits are requirements, not suggestions.

We will not

  • Profile individuals. Names are parsed in memory and only aggregates are kept.
  • Claim fraud. Flags read "statistical outlier" and list benign explanations.
  • Target communities. No lists, no message targeting by inferred caste.
  • Bypass access controls. Official APIs and permitted sources only.

We will always

  • Show uncertainty. Modeled values carry intervals and a "modeled" tag.
  • Suppress small cells. No composition output under 200 electors.
  • Use the crosswalk. No booth joins across elections without it.
  • Honor compliance mode. Opinion layers switch off during legal restriction periods.

What could go wrong.

Booths renumberedCrosswalk with a confidence score. Low-confidence links are excluded from swing.
OCR errorsTotals must reconcile with official constituency counts, or the page goes to review.
Sparse geotagsSentiment is tiered: booth, ward, constituency, district. Gray when data is thin.
Sentiment is not intentScore stance toward a named target. Present it as a signal, not a forecast.
Legal exposureCounsel review before any public release. Privacy controls documented in SECURITY.md.
