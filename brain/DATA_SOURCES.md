## Validation rules (every ingest)
1. Sum of booth votes per candidate == official AC total (tolerance 0.5%); else quarantine.
2. `votes_polled <= electors`; `valid + rejected + NOTA` consistent.
3. No duplicate (`ac`, `ps`, `suffix`) keys.
4. Booth point must fall in its AC polygon; else flag for review.
5. Log parse confidence per page; send low-confidence pages to the manual review queue.
